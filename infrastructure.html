Floating Dry Dock

Sadhav has entered into a landmark agreement with Mumbai Port to establish a state-of-the-art Floating Dry Dock in Bombay. This facility, aimed at enhancing ship repair and maintenance capabilities at the port, marks a major step forward in maritime infrastructure development. The construction contract has been awarded to Artson Ltd, a Tata Enterprise, ensuring world-class engineering expertise and execution for the project.

  • Length - 130 Meter
  • Weight - 31.20 Meter
  • Height - 12.70 Meter
  • Capacity of dock - 3800 ton
